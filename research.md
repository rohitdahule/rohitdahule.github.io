<!-- # Research -->

My research focuses on computational materials science using first-principles methods, structure prediction, and data-driven approaches.

## Main Areas

### First-Principles Simulation
I use density functional theory (DFT) to study structural stability, electronic properties, surfaces, and interfaces of materials.
