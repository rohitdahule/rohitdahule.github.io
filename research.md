# Research

My research focuses on computational materials science using first-principles methods, structure prediction, phase-field simulations, and data-driven approaches.

## Main Areas

### First-Principles Materials Modeling
I use density functional theory to study structural stability, electronic properties, surfaces, and interfaces of functional materials.

### First-Principles Phase Field Modeling
I work on parameter-free approaches for predicting alloy microstructure evolution using first-principles phase-field methods.

### Computational Alloy Design
My current work includes intermetallic and solid-solution alloy systems such as Ni-Al and Co-Ru.

### Surface and Interface Engineering
I study semiconductor surfaces and interfaces relevant to thin-film solar cells, electronic materials, and advanced interconnects.
